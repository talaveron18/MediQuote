## 150 words

Healthcare staffing quotes fail for reasons that arithmetic alone cannot catch: missing relief coverage, territorial holidays, fragile margins, ambiguous responsibilities or a contract that no longer matches the approved price. MediQuote Pro keeps its deterministic calculation engine as the source of numbers and adds an AI Review Loop around an immutable budget snapshot. Four specialist perspectives—payroll, finance, operations audit and legal—review independently. A severity-based verdict prevents a critical finding from being hidden by favorable averages. Users see evidence, disagreements, an adversarial prosecution and defense, deterministic absence scenarios, a contract and operational annex, version staleness and a SHA-256 review record. AI never changes a budget or approves it. The competition demo is reproducible without a key and clearly labels simulated behavior. A real OpenAI Responses API provider is implemented server-side with structured output, privacy filtering, per-user cache isolation and explicit failure states.

## Why AI?

Rules calculate known quantities. AI is useful for finding omissions, explaining relationships, comparing language and presenting competing interpretations—tasks that do not fit a single fixed formula.

## Why deterministic calculation still matters

Prices, scenarios and coherence gates must remain reproducible, testable and explainable. The model advises; code calculates and blocks.
